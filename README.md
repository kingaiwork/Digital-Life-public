# English

## Digital experiences should be able to remember who they are

Most digital experiences are temporary.

A session ends. Context disappears. A character forgets. A virtual world resets. A new application knows nothing about the history that came before it.

KINGAI Digital Life explores a different product category:

**digital identity and relationships that can accumulate history instead of constantly starting over.**

The vision goes beyond “AI characters.” It is about continuity itself — identity, memory, relationships, important events, changing state and the feeling that a digital presence becomes more distinctive because it has lived through something.

## Why continuity can become valuable

### Identity gains meaning through time
A digital identity becomes more personal when it is not recreated from zero in every experience.

### Relationships become deeper when history matters
A relationship feels different when past interactions can influence future ones.

### Worlds become more valuable when change persists
A persistent world can accumulate places, milestones, relationships and history that make it harder to replace with a new empty world.

### Characters become more distinctive
Digital characters become more memorable when they have recognizable histories rather than only surface-level personalities.

### Products can connect more naturally
Selected identity and context can create continuity across future experiences while each product still keeps its own rules and boundaries.

## Product directions

### Persistent digital identity
Users or digital characters can maintain a recognizable long-term presence across supported experiences.

### Long-term memory
Important moments, relationships and events can become part of an evolving history rather than temporary prompt context.

### Character continuity
Characters can become more familiar and distinctive as relationships accumulate over time.

### Living-world direction
Virtual environments can evolve through events, time, player history and changing relationships.

### Cross-experience continuity
Future products can share selected identity or life context without collapsing every experience into one giant application.

### Digital ownership of history
The long-term opportunity includes giving users a stronger sense that the identity, relationships and history they build are genuinely part of their digital life.

### Creator and entertainment opportunities
Persistent characters and worlds can support new forms of creator content, interactive entertainment, licensed IP and long-lived digital relationships.

## How Digital Life differs from AI Life and KINGAI Pet

**AI Life** is a playable second-life experience focused on people, relationships, home, career, city life and personal history.

**KINGAI Pet** focuses on companionship, collection, raising, growth and adventure with digital pets.

**Digital Life** is the broader platform/product direction around identity, memory and continuity itself.

The three products are related, but they are not the same experience.

## Why this could become a larger category

The long-term opportunity is not limited to games.

Persistent digital identity can matter in:

- entertainment;

# 中文

## 数字世界也应该能够“记得自己是谁”

今天绝大多数数字体验都很临时。

一次会话结束，上下文消失；角色忘记过去；虚拟世界重新开始；换一个应用以后，之前积累的一切似乎都不存在了。

KINGAI Digital Life 探索的是一个更长期的产品类别：

**数字身份和关系可以不断积累历史，而不是永远从零开始。**

它不只是“AI 角色会聊天”，更核心的概念是“连续性”本身：身份、记忆、关系、重要事件、变化中的状态，以及一个数字角色因为真正经历过一些事情而越来越独特。

## 为什么连续性有价值

### 身份因为时间才真正有意义
如果每次体验都重新生成一个“你”，数字身份很难产生真正归属感。

### 关系只有记住过去才会越来越深
真正长期的关系，必须让以前发生过的事情对未来产生意义。

### 世界只有保留变化才会越来越值钱
一个持续世界会不断积累地点、关系、里程碑和历史，随着时间变得越来越难被一个空白新世界替代。

### 角色会因为历史变得独特
数字角色如果只是表面人格，很容易被替换；如果它拥有长期经历和关系，就会越来越有辨识度。

### 不同产品可以更自然连接
未来不同产品可以在明确边界内共享部分身份或上下文，同时依然保留自己的玩法和规则。

## 产品方向

### 长期数字身份
让用户或数字角色在支持的体验中保持可识别的长期存在。

### 长期记忆
重要事件、关系和人生节点进入持续历史，而不是一次性 Prompt 背景。

### 角色连续性
角色随着长期互动越来越熟悉，也越来越像一个拥有经历的独特存在。

### 持续世界方向
虚拟环境能够随着时间、事件、玩家历史和关系变化而发展。

### 跨体验连续性
不同产品未来可以共享部分身份或人生背景，但不会强迫所有体验合并成一个巨型应用。

### 数字历史的所有感
长期机会之一，是让用户真正感觉自己建立的身份、关系和历史属于自己的数字人生。

### 创作者与娱乐机会
持续角色和持续世界可以扩展到创作者内容、互动娱乐、IP 授权和更长期的数字角色关系。

## 与 AI Life / KINGAI Pet 的区别

**AI Life** 是具体可玩的第二人生，重点是人、关系、家园、事业、城市和个人历史。

**KINGAI Pet** 重点是数字宠物、陪伴、收集、养成和冒险。

**Digital Life** 则是更广的“身份、记忆、关系和连续性”产品与平台方向。

三者相关，但不是同一个体验。

## 为什么它可能成为更大的新类别

长期数字身份并不只适用于游戏，它还可能进入：

- 娱乐；
- 虚拟陪伴；
- 游戏；
- 创作者社区；
- 互动 IP；
- 个性化数字服务；
- 长期角色体验；
- 未来虚拟世界和社交；
- 跨产品身份体验。

如果数字关系可以持续几年，而不是几分钟，它就能够积累今天一次性会话无法产生的情感、创作和商业价值。

## 最新智能体验方向

随着 AI 和多模态能力发展，未来数字生命产品可以更好地理解长期上下文、更自然互动，同时保持更稳定的角色身份。

重点方向包括长期身份、长期记忆、角色连续性、多模态、个性化事件、持续世界、创作者持续体验、跨产品身份以及长期记忆中的隐私和用户控制边界。

这些都属于路线方向，正式发布前不会作为现成功能描述。
