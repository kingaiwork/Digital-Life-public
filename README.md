# Digital Life

## English

**A long-term platform direction for persistent digital identity, memory, characters and evolving virtual lives.**

Digital Life is part of the KING AI main ecosystem. It represents the broader platform and product direction behind persistent digital beings and experiences that can maintain identity, memory, relationships and continuity over time.

### What Digital Life explores

- persistent digital identity;
- long-term memory and personal history;
- character personality and relationship continuity;
- reusable life/world state across experiences;
- digital companions and virtual beings;
- safe account and ownership boundaries;
- future creator, entertainment, social and cross-device experiences built on persistent digital life.

### Relationship to AI Life

**AI Life / 数字人生 / 虚拟人生** is a consumer-facing virtual-world experience. **Digital Life** is the broader platform direction that can support persistent characters, identities, memories and future digital-life products.

### Why it matters

Most digital characters and assistants are session-based. A persistent digital life creates a different possibility: the experience can accumulate history, relationships and meaning instead of starting from zero every time.

### Part of KING AI

Digital Life belongs to the KING AI main ecosystem and connects naturally with SEA's memory and identity direction while remaining a distinct consumer/platform product area.

# 中文

**Digital Life / 数字生命** 是 KING AI 主生态中面向**数字身份、长期记忆、角色连续性和虚拟生命平台**的长期产品方向。

它代表的不只是某一款游戏，而是支撑“数字生命能够长期存在并持续成长”的更广泛平台思路。

### 核心方向

- 持久数字身份；
- 长期记忆与个人历史；
- 角色性格和关系连续性；
- 可复用的人生/世界状态；
- 数字伙伴与虚拟生命；
- 清晰、安全的账号与所有权边界；
- 未来创作者、娱乐、社交和跨设备数字生命体验。

### 与 AI Life 的关系

**AI Life / 数字人生 / 虚拟人生** 是面向用户的具体虚拟世界体验；**Digital Life** 是更广泛的平台方向，可以支撑持久角色、身份、记忆以及未来更多数字生命产品。

它属于 KING AI 主生态。未来方向会明确作为路线图展示，不把尚未完成的功能写成已经全面上线。
