## English

### Digital experiences should be able to remember who they are

Most virtual experiences are temporary. A session ends, context disappears and the next interaction starts again. KINGAI Digital Life is the broader platform direction behind a different idea: digital identities, characters, relationships and worlds can be designed for continuity.

The product vision is to support persistent identity, meaningful memory, evolving relationships, long-term state and shared digital-life experiences across future products. AI Life and KINGAI Pet are consumer-facing examples of what persistent digital relationships can feel like; Digital Life is the broader platform and product category around continuity itself.

### Why continuity matters

- **Identity becomes meaningful** when it survives individual sessions.
- **Relationships become deeper** when past interactions influence future experiences.
- **Worlds become more valuable** when changes and history persist.
- **Digital characters become more distinctive** when they build recognizable histories.
- **Products can connect** when identity and long-term context are treated as first-class product concepts.

### Product directions

**Persistent digital identity** — A user or digital character can maintain a recognizable long-term presence across supported experiences.

**Long-term memory** — Important moments, relationships and events can become part of an evolving history rather than disposable context.

**Character continuity** — Digital characters can be designed to feel increasingly familiar as experiences accumulate.

**Living-world direction** — Virtual environments can evolve through time, events and long-term player relationships.

**Cross-experience opportunity** — Future products may share selected identity or life context while remaining distinct experiences with their own rules and boundaries.

**Creator and partner opportunity** — A mature digital-life platform could support creators, entertainment partners and new forms of persistent interactive IP.

### Why this can become a category

The long-term opportunity is not only “AI characters” or “virtual worlds.” It is a category in which digital relationships and identities become durable enough to accumulate emotional, creative and commercial value over years.

That can support entertainment, companionship, games, creator experiences, digital ownership of identity and new product relationships that do not fit neatly into today’s app categories.

### Commercial opportunity

Potential paths include premium digital-life services, identity and continuity features, creator platforms, licensed character experiences, entertainment partnerships, developer services and cross-product digital-life memberships.

These are strategic directions. No unverified revenue, user counts, partnerships or market leadership are claimed.

### Relationship to AI Life and KINGAI Pet

- **AI Life** focuses on the playable emotional experience of a persistent second life.
- **KINGAI Pet** focuses on digital companionship, collection, growth and adventure.
- **Digital Life** is the broader product/platform direction around persistent identity, memory and continuity.

## 中文

### 数字世界也应该能够“记得自己是谁”

今天的大多数虚拟体验都是临时的：一次会话结束，背景消失，下一次又从头开始。KINGAI Digital Life 的方向，是让数字身份、角色、关系和世界拥有真正的长期连续性。

AI Life 和 KINGAI Pet 是用户能够直接感受到的数字关系产品，而 Digital Life 更像是更广泛的产品与平台方向：围绕长期身份、记忆、关系、持续状态和未来数字生命体验展开。

### 为什么“连续性”重要

- **身份只有持续存在才会产生真正意义。**
- **关系只有记得过去才会越来越深。**
- **世界只有保留变化和历史才会越来越有价值。**
- **数字角色只有积累经历才会真正形成个性。**
- **不同产品只有把长期身份当成核心概念，才有机会形成更深的生态连接。**

### 产品方向

**长期数字身份** —— 让用户或数字角色在支持的体验中拥有可识别的长期存在。

**长期记忆** —— 重要关系、事件和人生节点成为持续历史，而不是一次性的上下文。

**角色连续性** —— 让数字角色随着互动逐渐形成更熟悉、更独特的长期关系。

**持续世界方向** —— 虚拟世界能够随着时间、事件与关系变化而发展。

**跨体验机会** —— 不同产品可以在明确边界下共享部分身份或生活背景，同时保持各自独立体验。

**创作者与合作机会** —— 未来可以扩展到创作者、娱乐 IP 和更长期的互动角色体验。

可探索数字生命高级服务、身份与连续性功能、创作者平台、授权角色体验、娱乐合作、开发者服务和跨产品数字生命会员。以上均属于战略方向，不代表当前收入、用户规模或已经签署的合作。
