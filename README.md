## English

### Digital experiences should be able to remember who they are

Most virtual experiences are temporary. A session ends, context disappears and the next interaction starts again. KINGAI Digital Life explores a different idea: **digital identities, characters, relationships and worlds can be designed for continuity.**

The product vision is broader than one game or one AI character. It is about creating a category where digital identity, memory and history accumulate enough meaning that experiences become more valuable over time instead of resetting after every session.

### Why now

AI characters are becoming more capable, virtual worlds are becoming more interactive and users increasingly expect software to remember context. At the same time, identity and memory are still fragmented across apps. That creates room for a broader digital-life layer focused on continuity itself.

### The product promise

**Persistent identity** — A user or digital character can maintain a recognizable long-term presence.

**Meaningful memory** — Important moments and relationships can become part of an evolving history.

**Character continuity** — Digital characters can feel more familiar as experiences accumulate.

**Living-world direction** — Virtual environments can evolve through time, events and long-term relationships.

**Cross-experience possibility** — Selected identity or history can eventually connect across distinct products while respecting clear boundaries.

**Creator and IP potential** — Persistent digital characters and worlds can create new forms of interactive entertainment and licensed experiences.

### Who it is for

- users interested in long-term digital companionship;
- players who value persistent worlds and identity;
- creators building recurring characters and stories;
- entertainment/IP owners exploring interactive characters;
- developers interested in continuity across experiences;
- brands exploring long-lived digital relationships;
- organizations considering future persistent digital identity scenarios.

### What continuity changes

Without continuity, every interaction must earn attention from zero. With continuity, value can accumulate:

- a character remembers shared history;
- a world reflects past events;
- a user identity becomes more personal;
- creators can build stories that continue over time;
- digital possessions or achievements can gain context;
- relationships become harder to replace because they have history.

### The experience ladder

**Identity → memory → recurring relationships → evolving world → personal history → cross-experience continuity.**

Not every product needs every layer. The long-term opportunity is to make those layers available in a coherent digital-life family.

### Relationship to AI Life and KINGAI Pet

**AI Life** — A consumer experience focused on a persistent second life, human-like relationships, home, career and personal history.

## 中文

### 数字世界也应该能够“记得自己是谁”

今天大多数虚拟体验都是临时的：会话结束，背景消失，下次又从头开始。KINGAI Digital Life 探索的是另一种方向：**数字身份、角色、关系和世界可以拥有真正长期连续性。**

它的目标不只是做一个游戏或一个 AI 角色，而是探索一个更大的产品类别：当数字身份、记忆和历史不断积累以后，体验会不会随着时间变得越来越有价值。

### 为什么是现在

AI 角色越来越强，虚拟世界越来越互动，用户也越来越期待软件能记住背景，但身份和记忆仍然分散在不同 App 中。这让“长期连续性本身”成为一个值得独立发展的产品方向。

### 产品承诺

**长期数字身份。**  
**重要记忆能够累积。**  
**数字角色越来越熟悉。**  
**世界会因为时间和事件持续变化。**  
**未来可以在明确边界下探索跨体验连续性。**  
**为创作者和 IP 打开长期互动角色机会。**

### 连续性会改变什么

没有连续性，每次互动都从零开始；有了连续性，角色可以记住共同历史，世界可以保留变化，用户身份更有个人意义，创作者可以写长期故事，关系本身也因为“有历史”而越来越难以替代。

### 体验阶梯

**身份 → 记忆 → 长期关系 → 持续世界 → 个人历史 → 跨体验连续性。**

### 与 AI Life / Pet 的区别

AI Life 是完整第二人生；Pet 是宠物陪伴、收集和冒险；Digital Life 是更广泛的长期身份、记忆和连续性平台方向。

更深长期身份、更强角色记忆、创作者持续故事、授权角色、跨产品数字生命会员、开发者服务、持续社交世界和适合的企业/私有身份场景。未正式上线能力继续标注为路线图。
