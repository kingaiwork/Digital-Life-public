# Digital Life · Market & Partnership Direction

## English

Digital Life explores a long-term category that can sit between AI companions, games, virtual worlds, digital identity and persistent personal experiences.

### Commercial directions

Potential future paths include platform services for persistent characters, premium digital-life experiences, creator tools, entertainment/IP collaboration, cross-product identity and memory services, device experiences and licensing where product maturity supports them.

### Strategic value

If digital characters can preserve identity, memory and relationships over time, they become more than disposable content. That can create stronger retention, richer personalization and new product categories across entertainment, companionship and intelligent experiences.

### Partnership interests

Relevant partners may include AI character technology, games and entertainment, creators and storytellers, device platforms, virtual-world infrastructure, identity/privacy technology and long-term strategic capital.
