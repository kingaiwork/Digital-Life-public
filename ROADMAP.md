# Digital Life · Public Roadmap

## English

### Now
- define durable identity, memory and relationship concepts;
- separate reusable platform direction from individual game experiences;
- strengthen public product boundaries, privacy principles and lifecycle language.

### Next
- richer persistent character models;
- reusable identity/memory experiences across approved products;
- creator-friendly character and story foundations;
- stronger portability and cross-device continuity concepts.

### Longer term
- persistent digital beings and companion ecosystems;
- creator and entertainment platforms built around long-lived characters;
- privacy-aware identity, ownership and digital possessions;
- broader cross-product digital-life experiences.

Roadmap items are directional until explicitly released and verified.

# 中文

### 当前重点
- 明确持久身份、记忆和关系的产品概念；
- 区分可复用的平台方向与具体游戏体验；
- 强化公开产品边界、隐私原则和生命周期说明。

### 下一阶段
- 更丰富的持久角色模型；
- 在经过批准的产品之间形成可复用身份/记忆体验；
- 面向创作者的角色和故事基础；
- 更强的可迁移性与跨设备连续性方向。

### 长期方向
- 持久数字生命与数字伙伴生态；
- 围绕长期角色构建创作者和娱乐平台；
- 隐私友好的身份、所有权与数字物品；
- 更广泛的跨产品数字生命体验。

路线图内容在正式发布和验证前均属于方向，不代表已经全面可用。
